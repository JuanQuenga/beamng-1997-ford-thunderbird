# Test the Thunderbird prototype

These tests require BeamNG.drive. Local file checks do not replace them.

## Test in Freeroam

1. Record the exact BeamNG version shown in the game.
2. Enable only the Thunderbird mod for the first test.
3. Load a flat paved map and spawn the V6 configuration.
4. Wait for the suspension to settle without touching the controls.
5. Check for shaking, a collapsed chassis, detached wheels, missing textures, and an incorrectly placed camera.
6. Drive forward and reverse at low speed.
7. Turn fully left and right while moving slowly.
8. Accelerate through all four forward gears.
9. Brake to a stop and test the parking brake on a slope.
10. Switch the lights, brake lights, reverse lights, and turn signals.
11. Listen for engine sound at idle and under load.
12. Drive over a small curb and inspect the suspension.
13. Make a low-speed front impact and a low-speed side impact.
14. Reset the vehicle and repeat these steps with the V8 configuration.

If the car fails to spawn or shakes violently, stop the driving test and collect the console errors. Do not buy it in an established career yet.

## Test in RLS

1. Record the RLS version and the names of other career add-ons.
2. Start a disposable test career or use a backup copy of a save.
3. Wait for the marketplace's normal stock refresh and check suitable used-car dealerships.
4. Purchase one configuration and check the price, fuel, and mileage.
5. Store the car in a garage.
6. Save, quit, restart, and load that career.
7. Check that the correct car, engine, transmission, wheels, and paint return.
8. Request a repair quote after a small impact.
9. Replace a wheel assembly in the parts shop.
10. Swap the V6 to the V8, then repeat the save and reload test.
11. Test refuelling, insurance, selling, and repurchasing.

Dealer metadata eligibility does not guarantee any particular listing or price. Other add-ons may change dealer pools or workshop behavior.

## Report a problem

Provide the following information with each problem:

- Exact BeamNG and RLS versions.
- V6 or V8 configuration, and the selected map.
- The first failing step and what happened.
- Console error text, or the relevant part of `beamng.log` in the active user folder.
- Screenshots of missing geometry or textures.

Remove personal paths from a log before sharing it. Keep the original ZIP available so an altered configuration can be compared with it.
